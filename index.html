Basic Strumming Patterns

Strumming gives rhythm to your playing. Start with these simple patterns:

  • ⬇⬇⬇⬇ (Downstrokes only)
  • ⬇⬇⬆⬇⬆ (Down, Down-Up, Down-Up)
  • ⬇⬇⬆⬆⬇⬆ (Classic pop rhythm)

Practice with a metronome to build consistency!

Practice Tips

✅ Practice 10–15 minutes daily rather than 2 hours once a week.

✅ Keep your wrist relaxed and fingers curved when pressing the strings.

✅ Tune your guitar before every session for the best sound.

✅ Record yourself playing to track progress over time.
